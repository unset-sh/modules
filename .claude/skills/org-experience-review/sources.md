**Research foundations and interpretation**

These sources informed the review questions. They do not validate the seven-perspective arrangement, agent count or report rubric; those are practical choices for this kit.

- [Ryan and Deci, 2000: self-determination theory](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2000_RyanDeci_SDT.pdf). Autonomy, competence and relatedness inform what to investigate. Specific controls and interface patterns are design hypotheses, not guaranteed psychological interventions.
- [Nielsen: ten usability heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/). Expert rules of thumb for visibility, familiar language, consistency, control and recovery underpin observable interface checks.
- [Nielsen: response times](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/response-times-3-important-limits/). Guidance for feedback and waiting; do not turn approximate thresholds into universal laws or infer speed from screenshots.
- [Hassenzahl, Diefenbach and Göritz, 2010](https://academic.oup.com/iwc/article-abstract/22/5/353/684432?login=false). Recalled positive technology experiences associated psychological need fulfillment with positive affect. The correlational evidence supports questions about meaning and expression, not causal promises for particular features.
- [Norman: emotional design](https://jnd.org/emotional-design-people-and-things/). Immediate appearance, interaction and reflective meaning provide separate review lenses.
- [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/). Accessibility criteria inform condition-specific checks. A partial agent walkthrough is not a full conformance audit or a simulation of disability.
- [Peters, Calvo and Ryan: METUX](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.00797/full). Distinguishes experience within interaction from broader well-being. Engagement alone is insufficient evidence of benefit.
- [Tuch and colleagues, 2012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2012.03.024). Experimental work shows that aesthetics/usability relationships depend on context. Assess visual impression and task execution separately.
- [SimUser, CHI 2024](https://doi.org/10.1145/3613904.3642481). Demonstrates potential for AI usability feedback within a limited smartwatch study; it does not establish equivalence to human participants across products.
- [Hämäläinen, Tavast and Kunnari, CHI 2023](https://doi.org/10.1145/3544548.3580688). Synthetic HCI responses can support ideation and piloting, with findings requiring human validation.
- [Mind the Sim2Real Gap, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11245). Conversational user simulation differs from human behavior; general model capability does not guarantee fidelity. This is evidence about conversational tasks, not proof of performance in visual UI testing.
- [Official UEQ resources](https://ueq-online.org/). For a later human study, use the actual instrument and handbook. Do not substitute generated agent answers for participant responses.

This kit uses first-person questions as review prompts, not as participant quotations or claims that agents experience the intended feelings.
